 Rainfall at 22 stations across northern India
Abstract: A research finding about the measurement of afternoon rainfall in northern India. The study was conducted at 22 stations across the region to collect data on afternoon rainfall patterns. The results show that the frequency and intensity of afternoon rainfall vary significantly across the region. The findings have implications for water resource management and agricultural planning in northern India.
Introduction: Afternoon rainfall is a common phenomenon in many parts of India, particularly in the northern region. The frequency and intensity of afternoon rainfall can have significant impacts on various aspects of life, including agriculture, water resources, and urban planning. Despite its importance, there is limited research on the measurement of afternoon rainfall in northern India.
