 "A brass compass is a simple, yet powerful tool used for navigation. It uses a magnetized needle that aligns itself with the Earth's magnetic field, allowing you to determine direction. To use a brass compass, first make sure it is not touching any metal object that could interfere with its accuracy. Then, hold the compass level and still, and wait for the needle to settle. The needle will point towards the Earth's magnetic North Pole, which is usually indicated by the directional arrow on the compass face. You can also use the compass to measure angles and navigate through the wilderness." The note also includes a diagram of the compass showing the needle and the directional arrow. A small drawing of a man hiking with a compass on his hat is also included.
