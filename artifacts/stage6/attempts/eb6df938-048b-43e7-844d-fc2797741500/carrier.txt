 pickering, cleaning, degassing, and storing. Demonstration of simple equipment used in the process.

This video shows the process of sorting apples at a small orchard. The apples are first picked and gathered into a container. Then, they are cleaned and wiped with a cloth to remove any dirt or debris. Next, the apples are checked for damage and any that are bruised or rotten are removed. The remaining apples are then degassed, which involves using a machine to remove any air bubbles that may be present inside the apple. Finally, the apples are stored in a controlled atmosphere to preserve their freshness and flavor.

The equipment used in this process is relatively simple and includes:

* A picket fence to support the apples as they are picked
* A bucket or container to store the picked apples
* A cloth or brush to clean the apples
* A degasser machine to remove air bubbles from the apples
* A controlled atmosphere storage room to store the apples at the proper temperature and
