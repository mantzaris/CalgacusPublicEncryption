 Part 2
Here is a continuation of the sorting process at the small orchard:
After marking the first batch of apples, we moved on to the next step: sorting them based on their size. The orchardist handed me a long, flat tray, and with a swift motion, pulled out a few apples and placed them on it. I watched carefully, taking mental notes on the size of each apple, and then followed suit. As we worked, the orchardist pointing out which apples to keep and which to send back to the bush.
The next step was even more crucial: inspecting the apples for any imperfections. Any bruises, scuffs, or signs of disease were carefully removed, which would not only affect the taste and texture of the apple but also put the consumer at risk. The orchardist took apart each apple, inspecting every nook and cranny, and I did the same, confirming or correcting their findings.
