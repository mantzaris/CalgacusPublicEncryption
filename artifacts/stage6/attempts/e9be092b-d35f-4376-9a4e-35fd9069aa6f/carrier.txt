 A brass compass is an instrument used to determine direction. It is based on the principle that the magnetic field of the Earth produces a force that interacts with the orientation of a magnet.Inside the compass is a small magnet called the needle that is free to rotate. It is attracted to the magnetic field of the Earth and aligns itself with the magnetic lines of force. On the face of the compass, you will find a second magnet called the dial which is typically marked with a series of degree lines or bearings. When the needle magnet aligns with the north-seeking magnetic field of the Earth, it points towards magnetic north. The direction of which is marked on the dial with an N or a red end.
