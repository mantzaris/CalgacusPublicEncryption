The compass is a remarkable instrument that has been used for centuries to navigate the world. It is based on the principle of magnetism and the Earth's magnetic field. The compass works by using a magnetized needle that is attracted to the magnetic field of the Earth. The needle is not fixed to the compass body, but is free to move and rotate in response to the changing magnetic field. The movement of the needle is what allows the compass to indicate the direction of the Earth's magnetic field, and ultimately the direction that the navigator wishes to travel. The compass is an essential tool for navigation and has been used for centuries to help people find their way.
