Sorting apples at a small orchard
It's that time of the year again! The apple harvest is in full swing, and the small orchard I help at is buzzing with activity. As the sun rises over the rolling hills, the air is filled with the sweet aroma of ripe apples, and the sound of rustling leaves in the gentle breeze. It's a magical time, and I'm thrilled to be a part of it!

As we begin the day's task of sorting apples, the atmosphere is lively and focused. The crew is divided into smaller groups, each assigned a specific section of the orchard to focus on. The goal is to identify the perfect apples, carefully selected and carefully placed into baskets or boxes, ready for storage or consumption. It's a labor-intensive process, but the end result is well worth the effort!

One of the most enjoyable aspects of sorting apples is the tactile experience. The apples, still slightly warm from the sun, feel firm and substantial in your hands. You can almost smell the ripeness emanating from them, as you gently rotate them to examine their color, shape, and texture. The varieties differ, too, with some having a more pronounced sweetness, while others possess a tangy, crunchy bite.

The sorting process demands attention to detail, as each apple must be inspected, and then either placed in the correct category or set aside. The categories vary, depending on the orchard's specific goals, but generally, apples are sorted by size, color, quality, and intended use (e.g., eating fresh, cooking, or making juice).

As the sun reaches its peak, the heat becomes a factor, and we take short breaks to quench our thirst and replenish our energy levels. It's during these brief moments that I get to converse with my fellow orchard workers, exchanging stories, sharing laughter, and enjoying each other's company.
