Title: A coastal report about waves around a stone pier: a case study
Author(s): Wang, J. ; Zhang, J. ; Wang, H. ; Li, Z. ; Tang, H. ; Liu, J.
Publisher: Coastal Engineering Research Centre, University of Technology, Sydney
Subject: Coastal engineering
Abstract: This report presents a case study on the wave behavior around a stone pier in a coastal area. The study aims to investigate the wave characteristics, including wave periods, wave heights, and wave directions, around the pier and to identify the impact of the pier on the wave field. A series of wave buoy observations were conducted around the pier, and the collected data were analyzed using wave measurement techniques.
