 a comparison of numerical simulations and observations
M. A. R. van Dongeren, S. J. H. Hulscher, M. H. M. Postma, and J. P. M. van der Meer
Received: 25 July 2006 - Revised: 24 August 2006 - Accepted: 25 August 2006 - Published: 21 September 2006
Abstract. Coastal morphodynamics of a stone pier in a wave- dominant environment is investigated using a combination of numerical simulations and observations. The pier is situated in a coastal area with a mixed sediment composition (sand, silt, and gravel). The simulations are performed using the 2D finite element model DELFT3D, while the observations are based on video recordings, measurements of the water levels and wave periods, and bathymetric surveys.
