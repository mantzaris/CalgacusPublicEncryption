A brass compass is an instrument used for navigation. It is a device that helps people to find their way. The compass uses the Earth's magnetic field to determine its direction. The compass needle, which is the small magnetized piece of iron attached to the compass, aligns itself with the Earth's magnetic field. The direction of the needle indicates the direction of the magnetic north pole and therefore the direction of the user. The brass compass can be used for a variety of purposes, including navigation during travel, camping, and outdoor activities. less
