 "A compass is a simple yet ingenious device that has helped people navigate for centuries. At the heart of the compass is a small magnet, called the lodestone, which is suspended in a liquid, such as water or oil." Note: Compasses were originally made with lodestones, but most modern compasses use a magnetized needle instead.

A compass can be simple or complex, depending on its design and application. Here's a basic overview of how it works: A compass has a needle that is free to rotate in any direction.
