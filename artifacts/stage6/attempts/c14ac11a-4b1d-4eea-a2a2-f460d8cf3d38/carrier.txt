In this three-part workshop guide, we'll take you through the process of repairing a wooden chair. In this part, we'll cover the process of disassembling the chair, inspecting the damage, and preparing the chair for repair.

**Disassembling the Chair**
Before you start repairing the chair, it's essential to disassemble it into its individual components. This will give you access to the areas that need repair and make it easier to work on each part separately.

To disassemble the chair, follow these steps:

1. Remove all the chair's cushions, upholstery, and padding. This will give you a clear view of the chair's wooden frame and make it easier to work on it.
2. Remove the chair's legs by unscrewing or unsnapping them from the main frame. You may need to use a wrench, pliers, or a screwdriver to do this.
