How to Store Fresh Peaches: Tips for Keeping Them Fresh and Delicious
Peaches are one of the most delicious and versatile fruits, prized for their juicy sweetness and velvety texture. But as with all perishable fruits, maintaining their freshness is crucial for optimal enjoyment. Here are some tips on how to store fresh peaches, ensuring they stay fresh and delicious for a longer period:

**How to Store Fresh Peaches:**

1. **Ripeness:** Store peaches at room temperature until they ripen. For firmer peaches, leave them at room temperature for a few days. For riper peaches, store them at room temperature for a few hours, then refrigerate to slow down ripening.
