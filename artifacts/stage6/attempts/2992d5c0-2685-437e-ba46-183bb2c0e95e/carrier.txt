Title: A coastal report about waves around a stone pier: measurements, experiments, and simulations
Authors: Tochai, Kenji
Nagata, Kiyohiko
Kurihara, Hiroshi
Date: 2012-02
Citation: Tochai Kenji, Nagata Kiyohiko, Kurihara Hiroshi, "A coastal report about waves around a stone pier: measurements, experiments, and simulations", Coastal Engineering, Vol. 63, pp. 1-12, 2012.
Abstract: The purpose of this study was to investigate the wave behavior around a stone pier using a combination of measurements, experiments, and simulations. A stone pier was built in a large wave flume, and wave measurements were taken at different distances from the pier and at various wind directions.
