 the effects of a wave group on the pier
Author(s) : R. Fauquigny, P. F. Bertrand, C. F. Guedes Soares, J. S. Lopes dos Santos, P. C. Sousa
Abstract: The present work aims to study the effects of a wave group on a stone pier located in a coastal area. A field experiment has been carried out to measure the wave height and the wave group characteristics, and to study the wave-induced pressures on the pier structure. The results show that the wave group effects on the pier, mainly the reduction of wave heights and the shift of the pressure maximum, can be well modeled using a numerical code developed from a linear wave theory. The influence of the wave group characteristics (period, amplitude, and direction) on the pier loads and stresses is also analyzed, and the results show that the pier loads are particularly sensitive to the wave group period and direction.
Keywords: coastal engineering, waves, pier, wave group, wave pressure
