The public rank-based transport comparator `public_utf8_rank16_v1` adapts the full-prefix consistency predicate from ImageCalgacus `imagecalgacus/text_backend.py:consistent_candidate`, the special-token attribute mask from its `TextBackend.distribution`, and high-nibble-first mapping from `imagecalgacus/fixed_rank.py`, inspected at commit `2bec65dbe5509623f6658d8a231ec93f6d579b4e` (MIT, copyright 2026 a.v.mantzaris; license retained in `vendor/IMAGECALGACUS_LICENSE`). It uses the already-attributed rank order/cache conventions from RankCloak. These helpers are implementation references, not evidence of correctness or novelty.
