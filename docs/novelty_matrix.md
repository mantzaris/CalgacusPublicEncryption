## What the measurements can support

The retained data support a narrowly documented **counterexample to treating exact encrypted-payload recovery as evidence of concealment**: a working profile can expose predictable public framing. That is a useful demonstration of an established distinction. The rank16 comparison with historical Calgacus failures is descriptive, not a randomized paired performance study. The two envelope fixtures do not repair old carriers or establish new-profile HPKE binding. Stage 4 historical checks are not new recoveries.

There is no held-out evaluation here. Six prefix controls per family cannot establish a population false-positive rate, and their fixed shared seeds do not produce independent operational traffic. A's latent token-path probability is not automatically its serialized-text match probability. The header observation belongs to `public_utf8_rank16_v1` alone.

## Comparator feasibility retained and clarified

The [`PublicEnvelopeCodec` and keyed interfaces](../src/llm_stego_public_key/codecs/interfaces.py) are contracts, not working arithmetic/keyed comparators.

- **Public arithmetic:** [NLS revision `14e98256…`](https://github.com/harvardnlp/NeuralSteganography/tree/14e982564aeaf9a33f7b4de440deda2184d17f12) uses a different model API, finite interval precision, token exclusions, termination and retokenization heuristics. An adapter must specify probabilities/order, actual UTF-8, bounded packet length and completion; it cannot inherit exactness by interface compatibility. ImageCalgacus's A1 is itself a labelled adaptation with reported text-capacity limitations.
- **Keyed reference:** [RRC revision `dae32625…`](https://github.com/ryehr/RRC_steganography/tree/dae326259e4fca8bc4fcf460dafdbc0e88a0a71a) has the noncryptographic CLI randomness issue recorded in the source review. A public fixed seed is not a steganographic key. Its shared-secret premise, synchronization, nonce policy and finite arithmetic need a separately reviewed implementation.
- **Meteor:** the inspected demo's secret key/nonce and PRG state remain part of its setup. A backend port and actual-text contract would require qualification. No code was executed here, and no security claim is inherited from an illustrative demo.

These gaps make a comparative systems paper more work than “add two baselines.” They do not authorize implementation, additional dependencies, experiments or budget use.
